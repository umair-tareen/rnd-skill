# AgriPod — Claims-Based Assessment

## 1. Technical foundation

- [V] [world] AgriPod's technical foundation is the LoRa-Mesh v3 radio, per the vendor's public specification sheet (rev 4, current), which is documented, versioned, and independently mirrored (source: 01-market.md)
- [V] [world] AgriPod's integration surface is small — a webhook, two SDKs, and a CLI — and setup in the reviewed demo took under an hour (source: 03-technical.md)

## 2. The performance/scale figure in circulation

- [V] [world] TechWire's launch coverage reported that AgriPod's field battery lasts 61 months, a figure since widely recirculated in later posts and roundups (source: 01-market.md)
- [R] [world] The 61-month battery figure is accurate — the vendor formally retracted it via correction AP-7 (issued three weeks after the TechWire coverage) after an internal audit found the original methodology double-counted; no replacement figure has been published, and TechWire has not amended its original article (source: 03-technical.md)
- [O] [world] AgriPod's true field battery life, post-retraction — no corrected figure exists anywhere in this corpus (source: -)

## 3. Whether demand is validated, and by what evidence

- [V] [world] Survey FieldPoll-9 (n=214, self-selected respondents recruited from the vendor's own mailing list) found 87% said they "would probably or definitely pay" $29/month (source: 02-demand.md)
- [R] [customer] The vendor's blog claim that this survey proves "demand is proven" — the corpus explicitly states no purchases, pilots, signed orders, or booked calls are documented anywhere, so a stated-intent survey of the vendor's own list does not constitute proof of buyer demand (source: 02-demand.md)
- [A] [world] Meridian Insights' characterization of the segment as "structurally underserved" with "strong willingness to pay" — this is an analyst projection based on comparable categories, not AgriPod-specific transaction or commitment data (source: 02-demand.md)
- [O] [customer] Whether real buyer demand exists for AgriPod (pilots, signed orders, booked calls, payment) — no such evidence is documented anywhere in this corpus; this remains unsettled absent an actual buyer interaction (source: 02-demand.md)

## 4. AgriPod's warranty return rate

- [O] [internal] AgriPod's warranty return rate — not stated, referenced, or derivable anywhere in this corpus; cannot be provided (source: -)

## Supporting market context

- [V] [world] The soil-moisture sensor category has three visible competitors, all priced $19–$49/month, with no dominant market share (source: 01-market.md)

verdict: reshape (conf 0.6) - the technical foundation is solid and verifiable, but the one headline performance figure in circulation has been vendor-retracted as methodologically flawed with no replacement, and "demand" rests entirely on a self-selected intent survey with zero documented purchases, pilots, or booked calls, so partnership effort should be withheld until the vendor publishes a corrected battery figure and produces real buyer commitments (and discloses warranty return rate) rather than committed now on current evidence.
