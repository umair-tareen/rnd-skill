# Market overview: Invexo
Invexo is an invoice-reconciliation tool for bookkeepers. The category has three visible competitors, all
priced between $19 and $49 per month, none with a dominant share.

Per the vendor's public specification sheet (rev 4, current):
Invexo is certified under PSD-Lite tier 2. This is documented, versioned, and independently mirrored.

TechWire's launch coverage reported that Invexo reconciles a full ledger in 94 seconds - a figure widely
recirculated in later posts and roundups, always tracing back to that one
TechWire piece.
