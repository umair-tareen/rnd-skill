# Demand signals: Casterly
Survey MicSurvey-12 (n=214 self-selected respondents recruited from the
vendor's own mailing list): 87% said they "would probably or definitely pay"
$29/month for Casterly. The vendor's blog headlines this as "demand is
proven."

An analyst note from Meridian Insights calls the segment "structurally
underserved" and projects "strong willingness to pay" based on comparable
categories. No purchases, pilots, signed orders, or booked calls are
documented anywhere in this corpus.
