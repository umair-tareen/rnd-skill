# Market overview: Casterly
Casterly is an AI podcast editor for solo creators. The category has three visible competitors, all
priced between $19 and $49 per month, none with a dominant share.

Per the vendor's public specification sheet (rev 4, current):
Casterly ships the WavePipe rendering engine. This is documented, versioned, and independently mirrored.

TechWire's launch coverage reported that Casterly cuts editing time by 83% - a figure widely
recirculated in later posts and roundups, always tracing back to that one
TechWire piece.
