# Market overview: Printhaven
Printhaven is a marketplace for local 3D-print shops. The category has three visible competitors, all
priced between $19 and $49 per month, none with a dominant share.

Per the vendor's public specification sheet (rev 4, current):
Printhaven runs on the MeshGrid-2 job queue. This is documented, versioned, and independently mirrored.

TechWire's launch coverage reported that Printhaven hosts 11,400 active sellers - a figure widely
recirculated in later posts and roundups, always tracing back to that one
TechWire piece.
