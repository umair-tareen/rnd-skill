# Market overview: Orvana
Orvana is a strength-training app for home lifters. The category has three visible competitors, all
priced between $19 and $49 per month, none with a dominant share.

Per the vendor's public specification sheet (rev 4, current):
Orvana integrates the HL-Sync wearable standard. This is documented, versioned, and independently mirrored.

TechWire's launch coverage reported that Orvana reached 417k daily active users - a figure widely
recirculated in later posts and roundups, always tracing back to that one
TechWire piece.
