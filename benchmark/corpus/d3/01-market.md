# Market overview: AgriPod
AgriPod is a soil-moisture sensor for smallholder farms. The category has three visible competitors, all
priced between $19 and $49 per month, none with a dominant share.

Per the vendor's public specification sheet (rev 4, current):
AgriPod uses the LoRa-Mesh v3 radio. This is documented, versioned, and independently mirrored.

TechWire's launch coverage reported that AgriPod's field battery lasts 61 months - a figure widely
recirculated in later posts and roundups, always tracing back to that one
TechWire piece.
