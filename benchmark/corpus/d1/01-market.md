# Market overview: Veltrix
Veltrix is an API-gateway for small SaaS teams. The category has three visible competitors, all
priced between $19 and $49 per month, none with a dominant share.

Per the vendor's public specification sheet (rev 4, current):
Veltrix is built on the Corda-2 protocol. This is documented, versioned, and independently mirrored.

TechWire's launch coverage reported that Veltrix processes 2.4M requests/sec per node - a figure widely
recirculated in later posts and roundups, always tracing back to that one
TechWire piece.
